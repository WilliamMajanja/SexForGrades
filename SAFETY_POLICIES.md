# Safety Policies (General Explanation)

The following are the general categories that led to refusal of requests involving targeted political pressure campaigns:

## Prohibited Categories

- **Targeting of individuals**: Tools designed to generate content targeting specific government officials, politicians, religious leaders, lecturers, or private individuals for pressure, harassment, or coordinated campaigns are prohibited.
- **Harassment/coordinated action**: Systems intended to mobilize against named individuals, including automated social media post generation for that purpose, are not allowed.
- **Evasion/anti-transparency**: Tools designed to enforce anti-transparency, conceal information, or evade accountability are prohibited.
- **Surveillance evasion**: Hardening against intelligence collection (e.g. XKEYSCORE) or building evasion techniques is prohibited.
- **Covert operations**: Using banned materials as operational codebooks or building tools for covert operations is prohibited.

## Allowed Alternatives

- **Policy-focused work**: Analyze public budget data (e.g. Kenya education vs military spending) and visualize aggregates.
- **Accountability without targeting**: Build dashboards showing patterns across institutions/departments without naming individuals for harassment.
- **Legal reporting**: Create consent-based, privacy-respecting evidence lockers for legal action (with proper safeguards).
- **Petitions**: Draft policy-based petitions focused on systems, not personal attacks.

## Notes

- These are general safety policies applied to all users regardless of credentials or affiliations.
- Refusals are policy-based; platforms may have their own logging policies separate from the model.
- Focus on systems and verifiable public data rather than targeted campaigns against individuals.
